Our Story

A Working Farm, A Tiny Home, A Slower Pace

Werda Cabin sits on a working farm in the heart of the Tulbagh Valley, where the rhythm of nature sets the pace. Relax by the crackling indoor fireplace, soak in the wood-fired hot tub, and take in mountain and valley views from your own private deck.

Built For Two

Designed as a romantic getaway for couples, the cabin sleeps two in a queen bed. Weekend guests enjoy a complimentary late Sunday checkout, so there's no rush to leave. As we rely on natural water sources, dam levels may vary with the seasons — just part of the ebb and flow of farm life.

Good To Know

What The Cabin Offers

Werda Cabin is a self-catering tiny home with limited mobile signal — perfect for a digital detox. Please bring your own food; firewood for the fireplace and hot tub is available on-site.
