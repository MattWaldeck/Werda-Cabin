Our Story

Life at the pace of the farm

Werda Cabin sits on a working farm in the heart of the Tulbagh Valley, where the rhythm of nature sets the pace. Relax by the crackling indoor fireplace, soak in the wood-fired hot tub, and take in mountain and valley views from your own private deck.

Built for two

Designed as a romantic getaway for couples, the cabin sleeps two in a queen bed. Weekend guests enjoy a complimentary late Sunday checkout, so there's no rush to leave.

Good To Know

Before you arrive

Check-in & checkout

Check in between 2:00 PM and 7:00 PM, check out before 10:00 AM. Weekend guests get a complimentary late Sunday checkout.

Built for two

The cabin sleeps two guests in a queen bed — ideal for couples.

Limited signal

Mobile reception is patchy, which makes it perfect for a digital detox.

Self-catering

Bring your own food — the kitchen has everything you need to cook.

Firewood on-site

Firewood for the fireplace and hot tub is available on the farm.

Natural water

We rely on natural water sources, so dam levels vary with the seasons.
